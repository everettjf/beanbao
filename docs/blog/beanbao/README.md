# BeanBao article source

This folder preserves the Chinese launch article and its original image assets.
`article.md` uses local `images/` references so the story remains readable and
illustrated when copied to a blog. `figure-plan.md` and `sources.md` record the
figure captions and evidence used while writing it.

The phone screenshots are development evidence from the single tested iPhone.
Some show an earlier UI label; the article identifies that difference. The
architecture PNG and two small app-icon illustrations are copies of the
versioned assets in `design/`, retained here to keep the article self-contained.
